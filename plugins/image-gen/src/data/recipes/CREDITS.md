# Credits for the built-in recipes

The built-in recipes in `builtin.json` are adapted from the prompt collections below. Each recipe names its source, the original prompt's author as the collection credits them, and the licence in its `source` field.

**Changes made** (all recipes): the prompts were turned into templates by replacing the specific subject, topic or wording with blanks (`{{name}}`) that have defaults; where a prompt spelled out the text for one subject (one city, one dish, one word), that text was replaced by an instruction to write the real content for the topic in the blank; names of brands, studios, franchises and living artists were replaced with plain descriptions of the style; aspect-ratio words and tool-specific flags were removed and the size set in the recipe instead; a few prompts were translated from Japanese or Spanish; recipes meant as animated loops describe one continuous motion; titles and summaries in Chinese and English were written for the Images plugin. Recipes from JimLiu/baoyu-skills were composed from that project's style, layout and prompt-template files rather than copied from a single prompt. The cover images in `covers/` were drawn for this plugin with gpt-image-2.6 from the adapted prompts (with fictional sample pictures for the recipes that work on a photo); they are not taken from the collections.
